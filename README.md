# 🌍 Problems Around The World

An interactive JavaScript educational game built with p5.js that takes players through 7 levels, each tackling a real-world global issue — from ocean pollution to climate change to COVID-19. Built as a submission for the BYJU Silicon Valley Hackathon (2022), placing in the top 6% globally out of 500+ submissions.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [rayhasgor07.github.io/Problems-Around-The-World](https://rayhasgor07.github.io/Problems-Around-The-World/)

---

## Levels

### 🌊 Level 1 — Ocean Pollution
Click to collect water bottles while avoiding paper and coke cans. Real pollution statistics appear as you progress — teaching players about the scale of ocean pollution.

### 🚗 Level 2 — CO2 Emissions / Road Trip
Dodge oncoming traffic in a driving game. Represents the environmental impact of car travel and fossil fuel emissions.

### 🐻‍❄️ Level 3 — Arctic & Polar Bears
Guide a polar bear across melting ice to catch fish before the glaciers disappear. Highlights the impact of global warming on Arctic wildlife.

### 🔥 Level 4 — Wildfires
Drive a firetruck and jump over obstacles to reach the fire. Raises awareness about the rising frequency and cost of wildfires due to climate change.

### 🚀 Level 5 — Overpopulation
Pilot a spaceship to Mars while dodging asteroids. Addresses global overpopulation with real UN statistics.

### 🪐 Level 6 — Solar System Quiz
An 8-question quiz on the planets in order from the sun — testing science knowledge with correct/wrong audio feedback.

### 💉 Level 7 — COVID-19 Vaccination
Move a vaccine to vaccinate 1,000 people in a hospital setting. Promotes awareness about the importance of vaccination.

---

## Features

- 7 unique gameplay mechanics across 7 levels
- Real-world statistics and educational facts woven into gameplay
- Sound effects for every interaction — correct answers, crashes, splashes, engine sounds
- Personalised experience with player name input at the start
- Smooth state machine architecture managing all game transitions
